HOW TO OUTRUN THE SKY

THE LOOP

Each day ends when you cover enough road. Fuel burns while the engine runs; storms and scavengers do not wait. Pull over at roadside pit stops, hold E to strip them for scrap, steel, titanium, chemicals and fuel cans. Keep the engine, chassis, tires and anchor rig alive.

TORNADOES & ANCHORS

Vortexes pull you in and lift the car off the road. Space drives hydraulic spikes into the asphalt: the pull is cut by an amount set by your anchor tier, and you take far less chassis damage. Anchors drain while they hold — repair them at the bench or with a field patch. Deploying at high speed shreds tires.

FIGHTING

F swings your equipped weapon at the nearest hostile in reach. Ramming at speed also works — armor and a ram bumper help. Raiders drop scrap, fuel cans and occasional rare parts. Keep a weapon repaired; durability only falls when it lands a hit.

MATERIALS

  • Scrap Metal — the base currency of survival. Everything needs it.
  • Steel Plates — armor, anchors, repairs.
  • High-Carbon Steel — katana-grade suspension steel, from convoys and mills.
  • Titanium Alloy — late-phase plating, hydraulic anchors, legendary blades.
  • Chemicals / Leather / Sword Cores — rare refinements for Tier 3–4 gear.

THE 100-DAY ROAD

Five phases: the Outskirts, the Sunken Highway, the Industrial Wastes, the Desert Canyons, and the Final Mile. Milestone days carry story events and tier unlocks; storm forecasts are posted one day ahead. Auto-save fires at every dawn.
