# 100 Days Outrun — Game Design Document

**Version 2.0 · 3D multiplayer build**
Genre: survival driving / action roguelite · Camera: third-person 3D · Players: 1–8 per room

---

## 1. Fantasy & pitch

You wake up in the driver's seat of a half-wrecked rig on the edge of Oakhaven. The highway out of town is
the only road left, and the weather has started hunting. One hundred days, one vehicle, whatever you can
weld to it.

The loop is deliberately **vehicle-first**: the car is your shelter, your workbench and your weapon. Getting
out is a risk you take for loot, not a separate mode.

## 2. Core loop

```
DRIVE  →  SPOT PIT-STOP  →  SCAVENGE  →  CRAFT / REPAIR  →  SURVIVE THE WEATHER  →  NEXT DAY
   ↑                                                                                    │
   └──────────────────────────────  storm warnings ◀──────────────────────────────────┘
```

* **Day = 95 s of real time.** Progress fills a bar in the top HUD.
* **Day counter drives everything:** biome, weather table, workbench tier, enemy pressure, boss spawns.
* **Death** ends the run (hp 0). The rig can be wrecked without killing you — repair it or walk.
* **Win** = reach Day 100 and survive 120 s inside the Category 5 Hypercane.

## 3. Player character

A blocky humanoid (head, torso, arms, legs, hands, held weapon) with a procedural walk/run/attack
animation and a floating name tag. Fully custom body colour in the lobby. Third-person camera orbits with
mouse drag, auto-recentres behind the rig while driving.

On foot: 4.9 m/s walk, 8.4 m/s sprint, 5.4 m/s jump. Can be pulled by tornadoes and hurt by acid rain.
Everyone in the room sees your avatar and chat.

## 4. Vehicle

Arcade physics tuned for reading at speed:

| Property | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Top speed | ~165 km/h on asphalt, ~61 km/h off-road |
| Acceleration | 12.5 m/s² × engine condition × fuel |
| Steering | authority falls off with speed; reverse inverts |
| Grip | off-road drag + tire condition |
| Nitro | +13 m/s, 2.6× fuel burn |
| Fuel | 60 L tank, ~0.18 L/s at full throttle |
| Damage | chassis / engine / tires; armor reduces 28 % |

Diagnostics are always visible bottom-left. Engine below 45 % emits smoke; below 25 % it barely runs.

### Anchor spikes
Deployable under 45 km/h from `Space` or the HUD toggle. Four animated spikes plus a heavier ring at
tier 3+. While anchored: tornado pull is cancelled, the rig is pinned, and the storm banner reads
*ANCHORS HOLDING*. Upgrade path: manual → hydraulic → heavy → titanium lock.

## 5. Threats

| Threat | Behaviour | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollow scavengers | Shamble at survivors, melee the hull or the body | Melee, run them down, revolver |
| Raider rigs | Chase and ram; drop steel/titanium/cores | Ram at speed, melee, kite them |
| Boss rig (day 40, 95) | 520+ hp armored rig, turret silhouette | High-tier weapon + a healthy chassis |
| Tornado Cat 1–5 | Drift toward players, radial pull + lift, chassis damage | Anchor spikes, altitude advantage, distance |
| Acid rain | Chips hull and blade durability | Stay inside, repair often |
| Heatwave | Engine degrades, fuel evaporates | Super fuel, engine repairs |

Enemy budget scales with day: up to 16 hollows and 4 raiders; spawn cadence tightens from 16 s to 6.5 s.

## 6. World

Endless highway corridor generated by recycling ~88 props and 10 pit-stops around the player. Five biomes
swap ground colour, fog, sky and light; a 24 h light cycle darkens the road so headlights matter.

Pit-stop archetypes: abandoned fuel stop, roadside diner, highway motel, military checkpoint, wreck yard.
Each holds 3 loot crates, refreshes after 12 s, and gives a different fuel yield.

## 7. Economy & crafting

Materials: scrap, steel plates, titanium alloy, high-carbon steel, leather, chemical compounds, sword cores.
Sources: scavenging (weighted by biome), hollow drops, raider wrecks, milestone rewards.

Workbench tiers gate recipes; fuel, repairs and armor upgrades are always available at the tier you own.
Weapon durability decays per hit and breaks permanently — the progression is meant to be re-crafted.

## 8. Multiplayer

Authoritative-by-owner model: each client owns its avatar/rig and broadcasts state at 11 Hz; peers
interpolate. Enemies stay local (per-client pressure) so a bad connection never rubber-bands a fight, while
the world seed, room code and day counter keep the shared fiction coherent.

Transports: WebSocket relay (Node), HTTP polling (serverless), BroadcastChannel (same-browser). Chat,
kill explosions and anchor events are relayed as discrete messages.

## 9. UI

Top bar: day/progress, zone name, speed, distance, weather. Top-left: sweeping radar scope (350 m) with
tornado, pit-stop, enemy and teammate blips. Top-right: chat + party list. Bottom-left: diagnostics.
Bottom-right: anchors, workbench, scavenge. Centre: prompt line + toasts; storm banner for alerts.

## 10. Audio

Fully procedural WebAudio: dual-oscillator engine pitched by speed, filtered noise wind, tornado rumble
that grows with proximity, and one-shot impacts, shots, craft chimes and alarms.

## 11. Tuning notes / future work

* Vehicle-vs-vehicle collision resolution (currently ram damage without rigid-body response).
* Server-authoritative enemies for competitive rooms.
* Weapon mods and rig skins, coop-only wave events, daily seeded challenges.
* Persistence of run stats (localStorage/leaderboard) and a photo mode.
